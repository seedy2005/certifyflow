# Community code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, accept good-faith corrections, and make space for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer.
